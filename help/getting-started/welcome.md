BRELA Desk keeps every client, case and document of the team in one place, and fills the official BRELA forms for you from the company's saved details.

## The idea in one minute

1. Save the **client** and the company's details once (paste the BRELA Company Details page and the app reads it).
2. Open a **case** for what the client needs (a change of directors, an annual return and so on).
3. Press **Prepare documents**, answer a few questions, and every form and resolution is filled in one go.
4. Check the documents, sign, and move the case through its stages until BRELA approves.

> Nothing is sent to BRELA by the app. People submit on the client's BRELA account; the app tells you what to do next and keeps the record.

## The menu

| Menu | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | The state of the office today |
| **My Work** | Your own cases and your team's queue |
| **Playbook** | Who does what, and how a case flows |
| **Case Board** | All cases by stage |
| **Clients** | Clients, company details, people and the vault |
| **Templates** | The Word templates the documents are made from |
| **WhatsApp Inbox** | Documents clients send on WhatsApp |
| **Doc Studio** | Edit and sign a document |
| **Reports, Settings** | Managers only |
