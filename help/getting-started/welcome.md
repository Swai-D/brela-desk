BRELA Desk is the office system of the BRELA consulting team. It keeps every client, every case and every document in one place, tells each person what to do next, and fills the official BRELA forms from the company's saved details so the team only has to check and sign.

## The idea in one minute

1. Save the **client** and the company's details once. The fastest way is to paste the BRELA Company Details page ([Import the company from BRELA](help:clients/import-from-brela)).
2. Open a **case** for what the client needs: a change of directors, an annual return, a new company and so on ([Create a case](help:cases/create-a-case)).
3. Press **Prepare documents**, answer a few questions, and every form and resolution is made in one go ([Prepare documents](help:documents/prepare-documents)).
4. Check the documents, sign, and move the case through its stages until BRELA approves ([How a case flows](help:getting-started/how-a-case-flows)).

> The app never sends anything to BRELA. A Senior or Manager submits on the client's BRELA account; the app keeps the record and tells the team what is next.

## The top bar

- **Search box:** finds clients, cases, documents, comments and vault labels at once ([Search](help:daily-work/search)).
- **? icon:** opens this Help.
- **Bell:** your notifications ([Notifications](help:account/notifications)).
- **Your name:** profile, lock screen and sign out ([Your profile](help:account/profile)).

## Where to start on your first day

1. Read [Roles and access](help:getting-started/roles-and-access) so you know what you can do.
2. Open the [Playbook](help:daily-work/playbook) and read **My role**.
3. Open [My Work](help:daily-work/my-work) to see what is waiting for you.
4. If you get stuck, press the **?** icon and search in your own words.
