The first time you open the **Dashboard** you may see a card called **Getting started**. It lists the first things to do, with a progress bar. It is there to stop anyone feeling lost on day one.

## How it works

- Each step **ticks itself** when the work is really done in the app. You never tick anything by hand.
- Every unfinished step has two buttons: **Go** takes you to the right page, and **Guide** opens the matching help guide.
- The card says how many steps are done ("3 of 8 done"). When all are done it says **You are all set**.

## The steps

| Step | Ticks when | Who sees it | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Add your phone number to your profile** | your profile has a phone | everyone | [Your profile](help:account/profile) |
| **Read the Playbook for your role** | you have seen the welcome | everyone | [Playbook](help:daily-work/playbook) |
| **Import the official Word templates** | the Word templates exist | Seniors and Managers | [Import the official templates](help:documents/import-official-templates) |
| **Add your first client** | the office has a client | everyone | [Add a client](help:clients/add-a-client) |
| **Import a company from BRELA** | a company's people are saved | everyone | [Import from BRELA](help:clients/import-from-brela) |
| **Create your first case** | the office has a case | everyone | [Create a case](help:cases/create-a-case) |
| **Prepare documents with a wizard** | a wizard has generated a file | everyone | [Prepare documents](help:documents/prepare-documents) |
| **Add your team members** | more than one active person exists | Managers | [Team members](help:settings/team-and-roles) |
| **Connect WhatsApp (optional)** | WhatsApp is configured | Managers | [WhatsApp setup](help:whatsapp/whatsapp-setup) |
| **Set up the AI assistant (optional)** | an AI key is saved | Managers | [AI settings](help:settings/ai-settings) |

Steps about the office (clients, cases, templates) tick for everybody as soon as the office has done them; the phone and Playbook steps are personal.

## Hide it and bring it back

- Press **Dismiss** (or **Hide** when everything is done). The choice is saved for **your account only**.
- To see it again open the **Help** page and press **Show the getting-started checklist**.

## Good to know

- The optional steps (WhatsApp, AI) are not needed to use the wizards.
- If a step stays unticked after you did the work, refresh the Dashboard. See what each step needs in the table.
