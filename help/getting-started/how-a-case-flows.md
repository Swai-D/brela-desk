Every case moves through the same six stages. The case page always shows **What to do now**: the current stage, who is responsible, the steps and the "done when" rule.

## The stages

| Stage | Meaning | Usually done by | Button that leaves the stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **New** | A request came in. Check the client details and assign the case. | Manager | **Start gathering docs** |
| **Gathering Docs** | Collect, prepare and upload every document on the checklist. | Secretary, Junior, Associate | **Mark ready for review** |
| **Ready to Submit** | A Senior checks every document. | Senior, Manager | **Submit to BRELA** |
| **Submitted to BRELA** | Submitted on the client's BRELA account. Waiting for BRELA. | Senior | **BRELA approved ✓** or **BRELA returned ↺** |
| **Returned (Correction)** | BRELA asked for corrections. Fix and send again. | Senior, Junior | **Corrections done** (goes back to Ready to Submit) |
| **Completed** | BRELA approved. Send the report and documents to the client. | Manager | none |

## What the app enforces

- To reach **Ready to Submit**, **Submitted** or **Completed** every item of the checklist must be ticked. Otherwise: "Complete all documents in the checklist first."
- Only a **Senior** or **Manager** can move a case to **Submitted**, **Completed** or **Returned**. Otherwise: "Only a Senior or Manager can do this step."
- **Returned** needs a reason. Otherwise: "Write what BRELA asked to correct."
- Each return adds one to the **BRELA round** counter, so the team can see how many times BRELA sent the case back.

## Two ways to move a case

- On the case page, press the stage button in **What to do now**.
- On the [Case Board](help:daily-work/case-board), drag the card to another column.

Both follow the same rules.

## Who is told

- When a case becomes **Ready to Submit**, Seniors are alerted.
- When BRELA **returns** a case, Managers and the assignee are alerted with the reason.
- When BRELA **approves**, Managers are alerted to send the final report to the client.
- A case more than 3 days overdue is escalated to Managers ([Notifications](help:account/notifications)).

> The steps listed in **What to do now** are advice. You can tick or skip them and they never block you. Managers can reword them in the [Playbook](help:daily-work/playbook).
