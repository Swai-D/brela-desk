Every case moves through the same stages. The case page always shows **What to do now**: the stage, who is responsible, the steps and the "done when" rule.

## The stages

| Stage | Meaning | Usually done by |
|---|---|---|
| **New** | A request came in. Check the client details and assign the case. | Manager |
| **Gathering Docs** | Collect, prepare and upload every document on the checklist. | Secretary, Junior, Associate |
| **Ready to Submit** | Someone senior checks every document. | Senior, Manager |
| **Submitted to BRELA** | Submitted on the client's BRELA account; the track number is saved on the case. | Senior |
| **Returned (Correction)** | BRELA asked for corrections. A reason is required. Fix and send again. | Senior, Junior |
| **Completed** | BRELA approved. Upload the approval and send the report to the client. | Manager |

## Rules the app enforces

- A case cannot go to **Ready to Submit** until every checklist document is ready.
- Only a **Senior** or **Manager** submits to BRELA or records BRELA's answer.
- Returning a case needs a reason, so the team knows what to fix.

The steps in **What to do now** are advice. You can tick or skip them and they never block you. The Manager can reword them in the [Playbook](playbook.html).
