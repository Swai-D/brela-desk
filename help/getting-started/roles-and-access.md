A **role** is a job in your team (Manager, Senior, Secretary, Junior, Associate or any role the Manager creates). Each role has an **access level**.

| Access level | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Staff** | Create clients and cases, upload and preview documents, comment, work the cases assigned to them |
| **Senior** | Everything Staff can, plus review and submit to BRELA, record BRELA's answer, change a case's assignee and due date, manage the vault, rename clients and checklist items |
| **Manager** | Everything, plus Settings, Reports, the audit log, the Playbook texts and the deletions |

Permissions are checked on every action. When a Manager changes someone's role, it takes effect immediately.

> If a menu or button is missing, it usually needs a higher access level. See [I cannot see a menu or button](help:help/cannot-see-something).
