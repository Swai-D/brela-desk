---
title: The vault (client logins)
summary: Store a client's BRELA login safely and reveal it only when needed.
keywords: vault, password, login, brela account, encrypted, reveal
order: 4
role: SENIOR
---
The vault keeps a client's BRELA online account login. Passwords are encrypted before saving.

- A **Senior** or **Manager** can add, edit and reveal logins.
- Staff can open a client's vault only while an open case of that client is assigned to them.
- Every reveal is recorded (who and when) in the audit log.

> Keep the server's `ENCRYPTION_KEY` safe. If it is lost, saved passwords cannot be read again.
