The **Documents** tab of a client holds files that are useful for many cases: passport and ID copies, the certificate of incorporation, signed authorizations.

## Upload

1. Open the client and the **Documents** tab.
2. Press **Upload** and choose one or more files. You can pick several at once.
3. The tab shows each file with its size, who uploaded it and when. A message says how many were uploaded.

The limit is **15 MB per file**.

## Preview, download, rename, delete

- Click a file name to **preview** it (Word and PDF open in the page) or download it.
- **Rename** and **delete** are allowed for the person who uploaded the file, and for any Senior or Manager. Others see: "Only the uploader, a Senior or a Manager can delete this".
- Deleting cannot be undone and is written in the audit log.

## Case files versus client files

| Where | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Client → Documents** | Anything reused: IDs, certificates |
| **Case → checklist item** | The document that answers one checklist line ([Checklist and files](help:cases/checklist-and-files)) |

Documents you filed from the WhatsApp Inbox choose one or the other: pick **Client folder only** to keep it with the client.

See also: [WhatsApp Inbox](help:whatsapp/inbox), [Upload fails](help:help/upload-failed).
