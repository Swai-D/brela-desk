The people of a company are saved on the client and reused in every case and document.

## Add or edit a person

On the client's **Company** tab press **Add person** (or **Edit** on a card). A person can have more than one role, for example director and shareholder.

The form keeps the details the official forms ask for: full names, gender, date of birth, nationality, occupation, ID or passport number, share class, shares, ownership, and the address in parts (country, region, district, ward, street, plot, block, P.O. box and so on).

> Forms such as 213a (appointment of a director) need the date of birth, occupation and address. The **Prepare documents** panel tells you which person is missing which detail before you generate anything.

## Add someone while preparing documents

You do not have to leave the case. In **Prepare documents**, choose **Add a new person** under a person question, fill the details and continue.
