The people of a company are saved on the client and reused in every case and document.

## Add a person

1. Open the client, the **Company** tab, and press **Add person**.
2. Fill the form. Only the **full name** is required; the more you fill, the fewer gaps in the forms.
3. Press **Save**.

You can also add a person from a wizard without leaving the case: choose **Add a new person** under a person question ([Prepare documents](help:documents/prepare-documents)).

## The fields

| Field | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Full name** | Everything. Written in capitals on forms. |
| **Roles** | Director, Shareholder, Secretary, Beneficial owner, Other. A person can have several. |
| **Gender**, **Date of birth**, **Nationality**, **Occupation** | Form 213a and other appointment forms |
| **ID / passport number** | Forms and the register |
| **Shares**, **Share class**, **Share value**, **Ownership %** | Shareholding table, annual return, call on shares |
| **Previous names**, **Other directorships** | Appointment forms |
| **Address in parts** | Country, region, district, ward, sub-ward, street, road, house, plot, block, P.O. box, postcode, unsurveyed area, landmark. The forms join them into address lines. |
| **Email, phone** | Contact |

A **surveyed** address uses plot, block and street. An **unsurveyed** area uses the unsurveyed area and landmark fields and replaces the street, plot and block on forms.

## Edit or delete

Press **Edit** on the person's card. Delete is on the card too and asks for confirmation. A person who appears in generated documents is not affected: documents are not rewritten when you edit.

## What the wizards need

The wizard warns you about missing details before it generates. For Form 213a (appointment) the important ones are date of birth, occupation, nationality and the address.
