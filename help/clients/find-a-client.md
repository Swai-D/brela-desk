Open **Clients** in the menu. The page has the list of clients on the left and the selected client on the right.

## The list

- Each row shows the client's name, their BRELA track number (or phone, or number of cases) and a small number: the **open cases**. It is green when above zero.
- Type in the search box to filter by name; it waits a moment after you stop typing.
- The list shows 10 at a time. Use the arrows under it to change page.
- The line under the title says how many clients you have, or how many match.
- Press **New client** to add one ([Add a client](help:clients/add-a-client)).

## The client header

Name, WhatsApp number (or "No WhatsApp number"), email and BRELA track number. Four counters: **Cases**, **Open**, **Completed**, **Documents**. Buttons: **New case** (starts a case for this client), **Edit** for the contact details, and for a Manager **Delete**.

## The four tabs

| Tab | What it holds | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Cases** | Every case of this client with stage, code, track number, who has it and documents ready | [Create a case](help:cases/create-a-case) |
| **Documents** | Documents not tied to one case: IDs, certificates, authorizations. Press **Upload** (several files at once). | [Client documents](help:clients/client-documents) |
| **Company** | The company's details, activities, share capital, shareholders and people | [Company tab](help:clients/company-tab), [Import from BRELA](help:clients/import-from-brela), [People](help:clients/people) |
| **Vault** | The client's saved logins (BRELA account, etc.) | [The vault](help:clients/vault) |

The number on a tab is how many items it has.

> Files a client sends on WhatsApp land in the [WhatsApp Inbox](help:whatsapp/inbox) and can be filed to the client folder from there.
