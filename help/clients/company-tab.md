The **Company** tab is the company's file. Every document and wizard reads from it, so keep it correct.

## What you see

- **The company card at the top:** name, number, type, registration date, registered office and contact details taken from BRELA.
- **Main business activity** with **+N more** when the company has several; click it to see the full list with codes.
- **Share capital:** authorised capital, nominal value and currency, and the number of shares issued.
- **Shareholding table:** each shareholder, shares held and percentage.
- **People:** one card per director, shareholder, secretary and beneficial owner ([People](help:clients/people)).
- **Other company fields:** everything else from BRELA or typed by you.

## Fill it

1. **Fastest:** press **Import from BRELA** and paste the registry page ([Import](help:clients/import-from-brela)).
2. **By hand:** press **Edit** on the company card. Add a field name and value, change a value, or remove one. Typed values survive a later import unless BRELA provides the same field.
3. **From a client's message:** on a case, **Smart fill** reads a pasted message and proposes values ([Case details](help:cases/case-details-for-documents)).

## Why it matters

Documents are filled from these fields. A missing registered office or a wrong share capital appears as a blank or a wrong figure in a form. After an import always glance at: company name, registered office, share capital, nominal value, the people and their shares.

## Fix a wrong value

Edit it here, not in the generated document, so every future document is right. Then generate the document again; it replaces the old copy.

See also: [Wrong company data in a document](help:help/wrong-company-data).
