In **Settings → Team & roles → Roles & access** each role is a card with its access level badge, **Team queue** badge if set, and the number of people.

## Add a role

1. Press **Add role**.
2. **Name** (2 to 40 characters, with letters or numbers).
3. **Access level**: Staff, Senior or Manager ([what each can do](help:getting-started/roles-and-access)).
4. **Summary** and **responsibilities** (one per line): shown in the Playbook.
5. **Team queue**: tick if people in this role should see unclaimed cases of their stage in My Work.
6. Save.

## Edit and delete

- The pencil edits any of the above; changes apply at once.
- The bin deletes a role, **only when nobody has it**: otherwise the app says so. You cannot delete the last Manager-level role ("Keep at least one Manager-level role").

## Messages

"A role with this name already exists", "Role name must be 2 to 40 characters", "Choose an access level", "Use letters or numbers in the role name".
