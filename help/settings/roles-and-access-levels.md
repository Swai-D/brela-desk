In **Settings → Team & roles → Roles & access** each role is a card showing how many of the permissions it has (for example "12 of 22 permissions"), a **Team queue** badge if set, and the number of people. You need the permission **Create, edit and delete roles and choose what they can do** to see this section.

## Add a role

1. Press **Add role**.
2. **Role name** (2 to 40 characters, with letters or numbers).
3. **What can this role do?** Tick the boxes, grouped as **Cases and clients**, **Documents**, **Client logins (vault)**, **Templates and AI** and **Settings and reports**. Everyone can already create cases, upload documents and comment, so those are not listed.
   - **Start from** Staff, Senior, Manager or Nothing ticks a ready set that you can then change.
   - **Tick all** in a group ticks the whole group (it then reads **Clear all**).
4. **Show the team queue in My Work for this role**: tick if people in this role should see unclaimed cases of their stage.
5. **Summary** and **Responsibilities** (one per line): shown in the Playbook.
6. Press **Create role**.

## Edit and delete

- The pencil edits any of the above. A change applies at once, even to people who are signed in.
- The bin deletes a role, **only when nobody has it**: otherwise the app says so. You cannot delete the last role that can manage roles ("Keep at least one Manager-level role"), and you cannot untick **Create, edit and delete roles and choose what they can do** on the last role that has it ("At least one active Manager must remain").
- Every change is written to the [audit log](help:settings/audit-log) with the permissions that were allowed and removed.

## Messages

"A role with this name already exists", "Role name must be 2 to 40 characters", "Choose an access level", "Use letters or numbers in the role name", "Your role cannot do this".
