**Settings → Audit log** ("Deletions and sensitive changes") lists, newest first, who did what and when.

## What is recorded

- Deleting or changing a case (client, type), a case type, a client, a file, a comment, a vault login, a template.
- Roles created, changed or deleted; team members changed (including password resets) or deleted.
- Importing a client from BRELA, importing or editing templates.
- AI settings changes.
- Creating, changing or deleting a document wizard.
- Signatures created, deleted or used, and PDFs saved from Doc Studio.

## What is not

Vault reveals are not in this list; the login row shows who opened it last. Ordinary work (ticking a checklist item, moving a stage) is in each case's **History**, not here ([Case history](help:cases/history)).

## Using it

Search the list for a name or a word when something has gone missing ("Who deleted this file?"). Entries cannot be edited or removed.
