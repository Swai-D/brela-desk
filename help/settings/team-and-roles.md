In **Settings → Team & roles**:

- **Add team member:** name, email (their login), role and a temporary password. They must set their own password at first sign-in.
- **Roles and access:** add or rename a role, set its **access level** (Staff, Senior, Manager), the responsibilities shown in the Playbook, and whether it sees the **team queue** in My Work.
- **Deactivate** a person who leaves. A person with work history cannot be deleted.

There must always be at least one active Manager. A role cannot be deleted while people have it.
