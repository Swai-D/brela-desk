Open **Settings → Team & roles**. Only Managers can.

## Add a team member

1. Press **Add team member**.
2. Fill **Name**, **Email (login)**, **Role** and a temporary **Password** of 8+ characters.
3. Save. The person signs in with that password and **must set their own** before the app opens ([Sign in](help:account/sign-in-and-sign-out)).

Messages: "Name, email, role and a password of 8+ characters are required", "That email is already used", "Enter a valid email".

## Edit a person

The pencil (**Edit person**) opens name, email (login) and WhatsApp number.

## Reset a password

Press the key icon (**Reset password**) on the person's row, type a new temporary password (8+ characters) and save. The person must choose their own at the next sign-in ([Forgot password](help:account/forgot-password)).

## Change a role

Use the role menu on the person's row. It takes effect **immediately**. You cannot demote or deactivate yourself, and at least one active Manager must remain ("At least one active Manager must remain").

## Deactivate or delete

| Action | When |
|---|---|
| **Deactivate / Activate** (the person icon) | When someone leaves. They cannot sign in; their history stays. Reactivate any time. Cases assigned to them should be reassigned. |
| **Delete person** (the bin) | Only a person with **no work history**. Otherwise: "This person has work history. Deactivate them instead: their history stays correct." You cannot delete yourself. |

> Always set the person's WhatsApp number so alerts reach them ([Notifications](help:account/notifications)).

See also: [Roles and access levels](help:settings/roles-and-access-levels).
