**Settings → WhatsApp** shows whether the connection works.

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Connected or not configured |
| **Webhook URL** | The address you give to Meta so incoming messages reach the app |
| **Send test to my phone** | Sends a test message to your saved phone. Needs your phone in your [profile](help:account/profile) ("Add your phone number in Settings → Team first"). |
| **Send today's digest now** | Sends the daily digest immediately to everyone with cases to act on |
| **Message templates to create in Meta WhatsApp Manager** | The six templates (assigned, returned, ready for review, approved, client document, daily digest) with their exact text |
| **Recent sends** | The latest messages and errors; "Nothing sent yet" until the first one |

The connection itself is set with server variables, not in this page ([WhatsApp setup](help:whatsapp/whatsapp-setup)).
