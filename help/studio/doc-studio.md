**Doc Studio** is where you finish documents. Open it from the menu, from a document on a case, or from a WhatsApp Inbox file (**Open in Studio**).

## Start: What do you want to do?

| Tool | What it does | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Sign a document** | Place your signature, stamp, date and text on a PDF | [Sign a PDF](help:studio/sign-a-pdf) |
| **Edit a PDF** | Click any text in a PDF and retype it, cover details, add notes and highlights | [Edit PDF text](help:studio/edit-pdf-text) |
| **Pages** | Rotate, delete, reorder or add blank pages | [Pages](help:studio/pages-tool) |
| **Images to PDF** | Turn photos of IDs and certificates into one clean, small PDF | [Photos to PDF](help:studio/photos-to-pdf) |

## Where are you working?

Choose the **client and case** at the top (**Search a client, case code or BRELA track number**). The tool lists the **Case documents**, the **Client folder** and the **WhatsApp Inbox**, so you can pick the file to work on.

- A file from your **device** is not tied to a client: you can edit and download, but nothing is saved to a case until you choose a client.

## Saving

| Choice | Result |
|---|---|
| **Save to the case** | Attaches the result to the case or checklist item; you may also **Mark this checklist item ready** |
| **Replace the original** | The edited file takes the original's place |
| **Save as a new file** | Keeps the original and adds a new file |
| **Download copy** / **Download only** | Only to your computer |

Saving is **Recorded in the case history and audit log**. **Unsaved changes** warns before you leave; **Discard changes** throws them away.

## Handy controls

Undo, Redo, zoom in and out, and the panels on the sides can be hidden for room (**Hide for more room**).

> Doc Studio works on PDFs. Word files made by the wizards are edited in Word.
