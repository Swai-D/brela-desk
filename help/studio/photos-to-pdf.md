## Steps

1. Open **Images to PDF**, or press the picture icon on a checklist item (the case and item are chosen for you).
2. Add images with **Choose images**, **Drop photos here**, **Take a photo** (back camera on a phone or tablet), or from the case folder, the client folder or the **WhatsApp Inbox** on the left.
3. **Drag to reorder.** Each image becomes one page.
4. Choose the **PDF options**:

   | Option | Choices |
   |---|---|
   | **Page size** | A4, Letter, Fit image |
   | **Margin** | None, Small, Large |
   | **Scan clean-up** | Original, Clean, B & W |
   | **Quality** | Small, Balanced, High |

   The **Estimated size** bar shows whether it fits the 15 MB limit.
5. Type the **File name**, choose the **Checklist item**, and tick **Mark this checklist item ready** if it should be ticked. Under the save options, **Keep the original images** is ticked by default; if you untick it, the photos you picked from the folder are listed under **Will be deleted** and are removed after saving.
6. **Convert to PDF**, check the **Preview**, and save ([Doc Studio](help:studio/doc-studio)).

## Tips

- Take photos in good light, flat, with the whole page visible.
- If the size is too big choose **Small** quality or **B & W** clean-up, or use fewer photos.
- Name the file clearly (for example "ID copy - John Mushi").

See also: [File too big](help:help/file-too-big), [Checklist and files](help:cases/checklist-and-files).
