1. Open **Edit a PDF** and choose the file.
2. Click on any text in the page. A box appears with the text; **retype it in place**.
3. Use **Cover details** to hide private numbers, **notes** to add comments and **highlights** to mark text.
4. **Erase marks** removes your own marks; set the **Eraser size**.
5. Save ([Doc Studio](help:studio/doc-studio)).

## Limits

- It works on PDFs with real text. A scan or photo has no text to retype: use cover and notes instead.
- Fonts are matched as close as possible. Check the page after editing.
- It changes the PDF only. A Word file made by a wizard is better corrected in the company data and generated again.

> Do not change the meaning of an official document. Corrections to legal wording must be made in the source and re-filed properly.
