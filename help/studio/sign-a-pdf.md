## Make a signature

Open **Sign a document** and look at **Signature** on the side.

1. **Draw:** draw with your mouse, finger or stylus. Choose the ink **Colour** and the **Pen thickness**.
2. **Type your name:** type it and pick the style.
3. **Upload:** a **PNG or JPG**. The background is removed for you; adjust **Strength** and **Tolerance**, or **Keep original ink colour**. Use **Remove the background** to try again.
4. Press to save it. **Make this my default** makes it the first you see.

You can keep **up to 12 signatures and stamps**: "You can keep up to 12 signatures and stamps. Delete one first." A signature must be a PNG image when saved.

## Place it

1. Click the signature, stamp, date or text item you want.
2. **Click on the page where it should go.** (If you forget, the app says "Click on the page where it should go".)
3. **Drag** to move; **drag the corners** to resize. Use **Bigger/Smaller**, **Rotate**, **Duplicate**, **Delete**.
4. **Repeat on all pages** puts the same item on every page (for initials or a stamp).

## Text and style

Use **Text style** for bold, italic, underline and strikethrough. Set the colour with **Pick colour**.

See [Doc Studio](help:studio/doc-studio) for saving to the case.
