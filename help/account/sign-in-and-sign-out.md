You need an account created by a Manager. There is no public sign-up.

## Sign in
1. Open the BRELA Desk address. You see **Sign in to manage your cases**.
2. Type your **Email**. Capital letters do not matter.
3. Type your **Password**. Press the eye button to show or hide it. Capital letters in the password **do** matter.
4. Press **Sign in** (or press Enter).
5. You land on the [Dashboard](help:daily-work/dashboard).

If you are already signed in, opening the sign-in page takes you straight to the Dashboard.

## Sign out
1. Click your **name or picture** at the top right.
2. Choose **Sign out**.

You return to the sign-in page. Do this on any shared or public computer. If you are only stepping away for a moment, use the [lock screen](help:account/lock-screen) instead.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What it means and what to do |
|---|---|
| **Wrong email or password** | One of them is wrong, or your account has been switched off. Check the keyboard (caps lock). If you are sure, see [Forgot your password](help:account/forgot-password) |
| **Too many attempts. Try again in 10 minutes.** | After 8 failed attempts from the same computer or network (or many failed attempts on one account from anywhere), sign-in is paused for 10 minutes. Wait, then try once, carefully |
| **Session expired due to inactivity** / **Please sign in** | You were away more than 15 minutes, or signed out elsewhere. Sign in again |

> The "too many attempts" pause is counted for the whole network, so colleagues in the same office who typed their passwords wrongly can also cause it.

## How long you stay signed in
- The app signs you out after **15 minutes without activity**. Moving the mouse, typing, scrolling or touching the screen counts as activity. Read more in [The lock screen](help:account/lock-screen).
- Even with activity, a sign-in lasts at most **7 days**.
- If a Manager switches your account off, you are signed out and cannot sign in.

## Common problems

- **I type the right password and still fail.** Retype the email too; spaces at the end of the email are a common cause. Use the eye button to see the password.
- **The page just reloads at the sign-in page.** Your browser may block cookies. Allow cookies for the BRELA Desk address, or try another browser.
- **I am asked to set a new password.** That is the temporary password rule. See [Your first sign-in](help:account/first-sign-in).
- **I cannot change my login email.** Ask a Manager. See [Profile](help:account/profile).

See also: [Change your password](help:account/change-password), [Team and roles](help:settings/team-and-roles).
