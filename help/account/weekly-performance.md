On your [profile](help:account/profile) the gauge shows **Completion rate** for the **last 7 days**.

**Rate = cases you completed in the last 7 days divided by the distinct cases assigned to you in that period.**

- It reads, for example, "3 of 4 assigned cases completed (75%)".
- Colours: red below 50%, amber from 50%, green from 80%.
- A case counts when it was moved to **Completed** (BRELA approved). If a case was reassigned, the person holding it at completion gets the credit.
- Cases whose assignment history is older than the tracking use their creation date.

The Manager's monthly version is in [Reports](help:daily-work/reports).
