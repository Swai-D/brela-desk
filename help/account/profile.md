Open your profile from your name in the top bar (**My Profile**).

## At the top

Four numbers: **My open cases**, **Completed this week**, **Overdue** (red when above zero) and **Active team members**.

## Personal information

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Shown across the app. Up to 120 characters. |
| **Phone** | Used for your WhatsApp alerts. Without it the app says "Add your phone number in Settings → Team first" when you ask for a WhatsApp test. Write it with the country code or start with 0. |
| **Login email** | Read-only. Ask a Manager to change it. |

Press **Save changes**.

## Profile picture

1. Press **Choose file** and pick an image.
2. Check the preview.
3. Press **Upload picture**.

The image must be a picture and **smaller than 3 MB**, otherwise "Choose an image smaller than 3 MB".

## Role and access

Shows your role and access level with a link to the [Playbook](help:daily-work/playbook). Your role and email can be changed only by a Manager in Settings.

## Change password

1. Type your **Current password**.
2. Type the **New password** (at least 10 characters, not a common word or number pattern) twice.
3. Press **Update password**.

If the current password is wrong you see "Current password is incorrect". If the two new passwords differ: "The new passwords do not match".

## Weekly performance

A gauge shows the cases you completed in the last 7 days divided by the cases assigned to you in that period (for example "3 of 4 assigned cases completed (75%)"). Red is below 50%, amber below 80%, green above.

## Recent cases and Team Members

The three newest cases in the app and the list of active colleagues with their roles.
