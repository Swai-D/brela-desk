BRELA Desk tells people about important events in two places: the **bell** in the app and, if the Manager connected it, **WhatsApp**.

## The bell

The bell in the top bar shows the number of unread notifications. Open it to see the latest 20. Opening the list marks them as read. Each item names the case; click it to open the case.

## What triggers an alert

| Event | Who is told |
|---|---|
| A case is **assigned** to you | The new assignee |
| A case becomes **Ready to Submit** | Seniors |
| BRELA **returns** a case | Managers and the assignee, with the reason |
| BRELA **approves** a case | Managers (send the final report to the client) |
| A client sends a document on WhatsApp | The team, to file it in the [WhatsApp Inbox](help:whatsapp/inbox) |
| A case is **more than 3 days overdue** | Managers, in the bell, once a week until it is resolved ("… is N days overdue, escalated") |

## WhatsApp alerts and the daily digest

If WhatsApp is connected, the same events also arrive on your phone, and every morning (about **07:00**, not on Sundays) you get **your cases today**: cases returned by BRELA, overdue, due today or due tomorrow.

You receive WhatsApp messages only if **your phone number is saved** in your [profile](help:account/profile) and WhatsApp is set up by a Manager ([WhatsApp setup](help:whatsapp/whatsapp-setup)).

## I am not getting alerts

1. Check that your phone is saved in your profile.
2. Ask a Manager to open **Settings → WhatsApp** and press **Send test to my phone**.
3. Check the **Recent sends** list there for errors ([Alerts do not arrive](help:whatsapp/alerts-and-digest)).
