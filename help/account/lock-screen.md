BRELA Desk protects client data. When you leave it idle, it locks.

## Automatic lock

After **15 minutes** without moving the mouse, typing, scrolling or touching the screen, the app opens the **lock screen**. The server enforces the same time, so a forgotten tab cannot be used later.

## Lock it yourself

Open your name in the top bar and choose **Lock screen**. Do this every time you leave your desk.

## Unlock

Type the **password of the same account** and press the unlock button. You return to the page you were on. To use a different account choose to sign in as someone else on the lock screen.

## The lock screen picture

The lock screen may show a photo of the period of the day (morning, afternoon, evening) and a short quote. This is only decoration. It needs optional settings on the server; without them you see a plain screen.

## What if unlocking fails

- A wrong password shows an error. Try again carefully.
- If the page says it cannot reach the server, check your internet and try again.
- If you forgot the password, ask a Manager to reset it ([Sign in](help:account/sign-in-and-sign-out)).

> Work that is not saved (for example a half-filled form) may be lost when the screen locks. Save before you step away.
