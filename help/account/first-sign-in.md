When a Manager creates your account, they give you your **login email** and a **temporary password**. The first time you sign in, the app asks you to choose your own password and then welcomes you.

## Steps
1. Open the BRELA Desk address given to you. You see the **Sign in** page.
2. Type your **Email** and the temporary **Password**, then press **Sign in**. See [Sign in and sign out](help:account/sign-in-and-sign-out) if it does not work.
3. A box named **Set your new password** appears with the text "Your account is using a temporary password. Choose your own to continue."
4. Type your new password in **New password (10+ characters)**.
5. Type the same password again in **Repeat new password**. Press the eye button on the right of a field to see what you typed.
6. Press **Save & continue**. The page reloads and opens normally.

Until you do this, no other part of the app works for you.

## The welcome box
On your first normal visit a box titled **Welcome, <your first name>** shows:

- your **role** ("You are Senior in this team"),
- a one-sentence summary of the role,
- **Your responsibilities**, a numbered list.

Two ways to finish:

- Press **Show me how cases flow**. It opens the [Playbook](help:daily-work/playbook).
- Close the box (X or outside it). Either way the box is marked as seen and does not appear again for your account.

You can read all of it again at any time in the **Playbook**, and your role is also shown at the top of [My Work](help:daily-work/my-work).

## Messages you may see
| Message | Meaning and fix |
|---|---|
| **The two passwords do not match** | Type both boxes again, exactly the same |
| **Password must be at least 10 characters** | Choose a longer password |
| **Choose a password that is not a common word or number pattern** | Avoid words like "password" and runs like "1234567890" |
| **The password must not contain your email name** | Do not use the first part of your email address |
| **Use letters and other characters, not only numbers or one repeated character** | Mix letters, numbers and symbols |
| **Set a new password first** | You tried to use the app before choosing a password. Reload the page and the box appears again |

## Tips for a good password
- Use 8 or more characters. A short sentence you can remember is better than a single word.
- Do not reuse the password of your phone, email or BRELA online account.
- Do not share it. Everything you do is recorded with your name.

## Common problems

- **I closed the "Set your new password" box.** Reload the page (F5). It comes back.
- **I forgot the temporary password.** Ask a Manager to set a new one. See [Forgot your password](help:account/forgot-password).
- **The welcome box shows nothing under responsibilities.** Your Manager has not written them yet. See [The Playbook](help:daily-work/playbook).
- **After a Manager resets my password I am asked again.** That is normal: a password set by someone else is always temporary.
