There is no "forgot password" link. A **Manager** resets it.

## Steps

1. Tell a Manager your login email.
2. They open **Settings → Team & roles**, press the key icon (**Reset password**) next to your name and type a new temporary password ([Team members](help:settings/team-and-roles)).
3. They give you the temporary password (not by email or comments; by phone or in person).
4. Sign in with it. The app asks you to set your own password ([First sign-in](help:account/first-sign-in)).

## Notes

- The reset takes effect at once and any old password stops working.
- If the only Manager is locked out, whoever looks after the app must help (for example with the server's database).

See also: [Sign in and sign out](help:account/sign-in-and-sign-out), [Cannot sign in](help:help/cannot-sign-in).
