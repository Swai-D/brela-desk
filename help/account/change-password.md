1. Open **My Profile** from your name in the top bar.
2. In **Change password** type your **Current password**.
3. Type the **New password** (at least 10 characters, not a common word or number pattern) and **Confirm new password**.
4. Press **Update password**. "Password updated" confirms it.

## Messages

| Message | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Current password is incorrect" | Retype the current one; if you forgot it see [Forgot password](help:account/forgot-password) |
| "The new passwords do not match" | Type the same password in both boxes |
| "Password must be at least 10 characters" | Choose a longer one |
| "Choose a password that is not a common word or number pattern" | Avoid words like "password" and runs like "1234567890"; a few unrelated words joined together works well |
| "The password must not contain your email name" | Do not use the first part of your email address in your password |
| "Use letters and other characters, not only numbers or one repeated character" | Mix letters, numbers and symbols |

Use a password only you know. Everything you do in the app is recorded with your name.

See also: [Your profile](help:account/profile).
