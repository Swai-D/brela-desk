When a client sends a file to the office WhatsApp number, it appears in the **WhatsApp Inbox**. Open an item, choose the client's case and checklist item, and attach it.

- A client is matched by phone number, so save it with the country code.
- A Manager sets up alerts and the daily digest in [Settings → WhatsApp](settings.html#wa).
- The counter next to the menu shows how many items wait for you.
