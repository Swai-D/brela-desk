## Messages sent

| Template | Sent when |
|---|---|
| **brela_case_assigned** | A case is assigned to someone |
| **brela_case_returned** | BRELA returns a case |
| **brela_ready_review** | A case becomes Ready to Submit (Seniors) |
| **brela_case_approved** | A case is approved (Managers) |
| **brela_client_doc** | A client sent a document, to file in the Inbox |
| **brela_daily_digest** | Every morning about 07:00 East Africa Time, not on Sundays |

The digest lists each person's cases that were returned, overdue, due today or due tomorrow (up to six, then "+N more"). Managers also get an in-app alert when a case is more than 3 days overdue.

## When messages do not arrive

1. **The person's phone** is saved in their [profile](help:account/profile) (with country code or starting 0).
2. **Settings → WhatsApp → Recent sends** shows the last attempts and errors.
3. Press **Send test to my phone**.
4. The six templates exist and are **approved** in Meta with identical text.
5. For a client who writes first, the 24-hour WhatsApp window applies; templates are used for alerts.
6. The digest runs once per day; **Send today's digest now** re-sends it manually.

See also: [WhatsApp setup](help:whatsapp/whatsapp-setup), [Notifications](help:account/notifications).
