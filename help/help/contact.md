---
1. **Search this Help** with the words of your problem or the message you see ([Error messages](help:help/slow-or-error-messages)).
2. **Ask your Manager.** They can see Settings, the audit log and your role.
3. For a fault in the app, write down:
   - the page and what you pressed,
   - the exact message,
   - the client or case code,
   - the time,

   and send it to the person who looks after BRELA Desk.

> Never send passwords. Share case codes and screenshots instead.
