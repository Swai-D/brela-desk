Documents never invent data: they copy the company file. Fix the source, then generate again.

| Wrong | Fix it in |
|---|---|
| Company name, number, registered office | Client → **Company** tab → **Edit** (or import again) |
| Share capital, nominal value, shares issued | Company tab → company fields; shareholders' shares on each person |
| A person's name, date of birth, address | Company tab → the person's **Edit** |
| Who is a director/shareholder/secretary | The person's **Roles** |
| A date, amount or choice in a wizard | Reopen the wizard, change the answer |
| Case-specific facts | Case → **Details for documents** |
| Articles of Association references | The wizard's last section; they are saved on the client |

If the BRELA register itself is out of date, import after BRELA updates it ([Import](help:clients/import-from-brela)). Typed values that BRELA does not provide are kept when you import again.

> After fixing, press **Generate** again; the old file is replaced.
