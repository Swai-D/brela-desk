## BRELA and company words

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **BRELA** | Business Registrations and Licensing Agency, where companies are registered |
| **Track number** | The number BRELA gives to an application so you can follow it |
| **Registered office** | The company's official address |
| **MEMARTS** | Memorandum and Articles of Association: the company's constitution |
| **Articles of Association** | The internal rules (meetings, quorum, directors). The wizards ask for the article numbers |
| **Table A** | The standard set of articles a company adopts when it has none of its own |
| **Resolution** | A formal written decision. Members' resolution: by shareholders. Directors' resolution: by directors. |
| **Quorum** | The minimum number of people at a meeting for it to be valid |
| **Nominal value** | The face value of one share, set when the company is formed |
| **Share capital** | The total value of shares the company may issue (authorised) or has issued |
| **Allotment** | Issuing shares to a person |
| **Allottee** | The person receiving the shares |
| **Call on shares** | A request that shareholders pay an unpaid part of their shares |
| **Notice of call** | The letter telling a member how much to pay and by when |
| **Reg. 29 notice** | A notice that warns payment is overdue and shares may be forfeited |
| **Forfeiture** | Taking back the shares of a member who did not pay |
| **Re-allotment** | Issuing forfeited shares to someone else |
| **Regulation 95** | A rule that stops directors being interested in contracts; relaxed by a members' resolution when all directors are allottees |
| **Beneficial owner** | The real person who owns or controls the company |
| **Director / Secretary / Shareholder** | The company's roles |
| **Annual return** | The yearly report to BRELA (Form 131) |
| **s.189** | The section of the Companies Act requiring at least 2 directors in a private company |

## Forms

| Form | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Form 213a** | Appointment of a director or secretary |
| **Form 213b** | Termination (resignation or removal) of a director or secretary |
| **Form 57a** | Return of allotment of shares |
| **Form 57b** | Contract for shares not paid in cash |
| **Form 131** | Annual return |

## App words

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Case** | One piece of work for a client, with a checklist and a stage |
| **Stage** | New, Gathering Docs, Ready to Submit, Submitted to BRELA, Returned (Correction), Completed |
| **Checklist** | The documents a case needs |
| **BRELA round** | How many times BRELA has returned the case |
| **Role / Permissions** | The job / the tick boxes that say what the job may do (Staff, Senior and Manager are starting sets) |
| **Team queue** | Unclaimed cases of a stage shown to roles that handle it |
| **Vault** | Encrypted client logins |
| **Wizard** | A guided set of questions that makes several documents at once |
| **Template** | A Word or text layout documents are made from |
| **Placeholder / field** | A spot in a template filled with a detail, such as `{{company_name}}` |
| **Gap** | A placeholder that could not be filled and needs a person |
| **Approved / Draft template** | Approved fills instantly; draft may use the AI |
| **Smart fill** | Reads a pasted message to propose details (uses the AI) |
| **Needs check / Checked by** | The review status of a generated document: a second person has to read it and press **Mark as checked** |
| **Doc Studio** | The PDF tools for signing, editing and photos |
| **Digest** | The morning WhatsApp message with your cases for the day |
| **Audit log** | The Manager's record of deletions and sensitive changes |
