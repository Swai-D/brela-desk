Most often your role does not have the permission ticked.

| You are missing | It needs |
|---|---|
| **Settings**, **Reports** in the menu | Manager |
| **Edit case**, assignee and due date | Senior or Manager |
| **Submit to BRELA**, **BRELA approved**, **BRELA returned** | Senior or Manager |
| **Add login**, edit or delete a login in the vault | Senior or Manager |
| Rename a client (the name box is grey) | Senior or Manager |
| Rename or remove checklist items | Senior or Manager |
| Edit, approve or delete templates; **Import Word package** | Senior or Manager |
| Delete a client or a case | Manager |
| A vault login you can reveal | Senior, Manager, or Staff with an open case of that client assigned to you |
| **Document wizards** pane | Manager |

## What to do

1. Open [the Playbook](help:daily-work/playbook), tab **My role**, to see what your role can and cannot do.
2. Ask a Manager to change your role if you need more ([Team and roles](help:settings/team-and-roles)). It takes effect immediately; refresh the page.
3. If you only need a one-off action, ask a Senior or Manager to do it.

> If the menu looks different after a role change, press **Ctrl+F5** to refresh.

See also: [Roles and access](help:getting-started/roles-and-access).
