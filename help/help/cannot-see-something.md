Most often it needs a higher access level.

- **Settings** and **Reports** are for Managers.
- Submitting to BRELA, recording BRELA's answer and changing a case's assignee or due date are for Seniors and Managers.
- The vault opens for Staff only while an open case of that client is assigned to them.

Ask your Manager to check your role, see [Roles and access](help:getting-started/roles-and-access). Changes take effect immediately.
