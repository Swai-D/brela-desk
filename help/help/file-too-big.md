Files are limited to **15 MB**.

## For photos and scans

1. Open **Doc Studio → Images to PDF** ([Photos to PDF](help:studio/photos-to-pdf)).
2. Add the photos, choose **Quality: Small** and **Scan clean-up: B & W** or **Clean**.
3. Watch **Estimated size** until the bar fits, then convert.

## For a big PDF

Scan at a lower resolution, or split it into two files (for example "Annual accounts part 1" and "part 2") and upload each to its own checklist item or as extra items.

## For Word files

Remove large pictures or compress them in Word (Picture format → Compress).

> WhatsApp compresses photos by itself; files above 15 MB are not received.
