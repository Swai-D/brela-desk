summary: Common reasons a wizard shows warnings or leaves gaps.
keywords: wizard problem, templates not imported, missing details, gaps, wrong name, blank, warning, not saved yet
order: 2
---
| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "The official Word templates are not imported yet" | A Senior or Manager opens **Templates** and presses **Import** |
| A person is listed as "not saved yet" | Add them on the client's Company tab, or use **Add a new person** in the wizard |
| A person is listed with missing details | Edit the person and fill what is listed (date of birth, occupation, address) |
| A company detail is blank in the form | Import the company from BRELA again, or edit the Company tab |
| A warning about directors, notice days or a call amount | Read it: it is a legal check. Change the answer, or continue if you are sure |

See [Prepare documents](help:documents/prepare-documents).
