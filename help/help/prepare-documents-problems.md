| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "The official Word templates are not imported yet" | A Senior or Manager: **Templates → Import Word package** |
| A person listed as "not saved yet" | Add them on the client's Company tab, or use **Add a new person** in the wizard |
| A person with missing details | Edit the person and fill what is listed (date of birth, occupation, address) |
| "After these changes the company would have N directors…" | Appoint before anyone resigns; a private company needs at least 2 |
| "The company has no saved secretary to end." | Give the current secretary the role **Secretary** on the Company tab |
| "A call may not be more than one quarter of the nominal value" | Lower the call per share |
| "The payment date must be at least 14 clear days after the notice" | Move the due date later |
| "The allottees receive N shares but only N were forfeited." | Fix the allottees' shares |
| A company detail is blank in the form | Import again from BRELA or edit the Company tab |
| A wizard you expected is missing | A Manager may have turned it off ([Wizards](help:settings/document-wizards)) |

See also: [Prepare documents](help:documents/prepare-documents), [Wizard guides](help:documents/wizard-change-of-directors).
