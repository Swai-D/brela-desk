## What comes from where

- **From the Company tab:** company name, postal address, phone, email, registration number and date, TIN, accounting date, owners with nationality and shares (the percentages are calculated), directors, secretary, share capital, number of shares and nominal value.
- **You type:** the factory, products, raw materials, investment, staff and cost assumptions.
- **The app calculates:** every total, the weighted average price, the shareholder loans (invested capital minus share capital), the output and revenue for each year, and EBITDA. The AI never writes a figure.
- **The AI can draft:** only the wording sections. See "Draft with AI" below.

## Questions

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Factory location** (required), **TRA tax office** (required), **TIN** | An empty TIN, telephone, email or accounting date uses the Company tab. |
| **Main business activity**, **Supporting activities**, **Products** (required) | Empty main activity uses the activity saved on the Company tab. |
| **Products and annual capacity at full capacity (up to 4)** (required) | Product name, unit, units per year, price at factory. The capacity per year is calculated. |
| **Production capacity (words)**, **Note under the products table** | Capacity is written for you when empty. |
| **Location advantage**, **Premises status** (required), **Required raw materials**, **Source of materials** | **Premises status** must describe how the site is really held. |
| **Annual raw material requirements at full capacity (up to 10)** (required) | Origin (Local or Imported), raw material, unit, location, number of units, price per unit. |
| **Allocation of invested capital (up to 8)** (required) | Item and amount. The total is the sum. |
| **Local staff**, **Foreign experts**, **Permanent positions**, **Temporary / contract positions** | Staff numbers by sex, what they work on, and the type of positions. |
| **Types of waste produced**, **Waste management and action** | Wording. |
| **Marketing: target market**, **Marketing strategy points** | One point per line, up to 6. |
| **Capacity utilisation, year 1, 2 and 3 (%)** | Default 50, 70 and 85. |
| **Other variable cost per unit (TZS)**, **Fixed costs per year (TZS)** | Used to calculate EBITDA on top of the raw materials. |
| **Payback period** | Your own estimate, for example "4 to 5 years". The sentence is left out when empty. |
| **Economic benefits (up to 6)** (required) | Heading and text. |

Use **+ Add row** to add a table row and **×** to remove one. Rows left empty are taken out of the document.

## Draft with AI

The **Draft with AI** button writes the wording sections (location advantage, raw materials, source, wastes, marketing and economic benefits) from your answers. It fills only the empty sections; if all have text it asks before replacing them. It follows the **Business plan prompt** in Settings → AI assistant, which holds the instructions and examples. A Manager can edit that prompt or reset it to the recommended one.

The AI writes words only. **Read and edit every section** before you generate: check the agencies, laws and standards it names, and remove anything that is not true for this client.

## Warnings

- "The company has no saved share capital": the authorised capital and the shareholder loans stay highlighted. Save it on the **Company** tab.
- "The invested capital is less than the authorised share capital."
- A shareholder with no nationality or no shares recorded, or no shareholders saved.
- "No TIN was typed and none is saved": the registration line is written without it.
- "Permanent plus temporary positions do not equal the total staff."
- "Capacity utilisation ... is above 100%."
- "Enter the other variable cost per unit and the fixed costs per year, or the EBITDA stays highlighted."
- "The calculated EBITDA is negative in at least one year."
- A reference payback (investment divided by the year 3 EBITDA) to compare with the period you wrote.
- "Some wording sections are empty."

## Tips

- Check the figures against the client's own documents before submitting to BRELA.
- After BRELA accepts the document, a second person checks it on the case as with every generated document.
