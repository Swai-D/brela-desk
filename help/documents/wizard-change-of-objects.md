## Questions

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| **The objects as they will read after the change** (required) | Write the new objects in full, numbered. |
| **The altered Memorandum is prepared to attach to Form 09** (required) | Tick to confirm. |
| **Members' meeting: date** (required) | The date of the meeting. |
| **Time** | Default 10:00. |
| **Chairman of the meeting** | Defaults to the first director. |
| **Notice of the meeting** | **Full notice (not less than 21 days)** or **Shorter notice agreed by the members**. |
| **Full notice: date it was given. Shorter notice: date the members agreed** (required) | Goes into the notice paragraph of the resolution. |
| **Director who signs the form** | Defaults to the first director. |
| **Date the director signs** | Defaults to the meeting date. |

## Documents made

- **Members' special resolution: change of company objects** (section 9 and section 146).
- **Form 09: change of company objects.**

These resolutions are drafted from the house style and the Companies Act, and are marked in the package as pending the senior's review. Read each one once before the team relies on it.

## Warnings

- "Full notice must be at least 21 days before the meeting." Choose shorter notice if the members agreed in writing, or move the meeting date.
- "The notice date is after the meeting date."
- A member with no shares recorded: correct it on the **Company** tab, or the resolution shows 0 shares.

## Tips

- Members who hold at least the share of capital set out in section 9(3) may object to the court within 30 days. Deliver the altered Memorandum to the Registrar within 14 days after that period (section 9(9)).
