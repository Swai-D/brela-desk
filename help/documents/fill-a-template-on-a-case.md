This is for one document at a time (the wizards make several at once).

> **Official forms, resolutions and your own Word templates use the wizard.** When you choose an official template (Forms 131, 213a, 213b, 57a, 57b and the resolutions and notices) or a template made from your own Word file, **Prepare** changes to **Answer the questions** and opens the matching wizard of [Prepare documents](help:documents/prepare-documents). You get exactly the same document wherever you start. Only your own templates follow the steps below.

## Steps

1. On a case, press the **robot icon** on a checklist item, or **Prepare a document** above the list.
2. Choose the **Template** (items suggest one from its match words). Name the **Document name**.
3. Press **Prepare** (**Answer the questions** for an official template).
   - **Approved** template: **No AI used**, filled instantly.
   - **Draft** template: the AI prepares it using the template as the format guide. Tick **Use the AI even though this template is approved** to regenerate an approved one.
   - **No template**: the AI writes it in the standard layout.
4. In **Fill the gaps**, type the missing details. Tick **Remember these on this case** so next time they are filled automatically.
5. Read the text carefully ("the AI can make mistakes") and edit it.
6. Press **Save to case as Word (.docx)**. The file is attached to the item, which is ticked when no gaps remain. A PDF copy is saved next to it, and the message shows **Download Word** and **Download PDF**.
7. Optional: **Teach the template from my corrected version** ([Versions](help:documents/template-versions-and-approval)).

## When a document is made by a wizard

If a template is made by a wizard, the window closes and the wizard opens at once. If a document still needs answers (for example a required question was never answered), a dialog says which wizard it is and lists what is missing. Press **Open <wizard name>** to go straight there, or **Not now** to stay on the case. You never have to look for the wizard yourself.

## Privacy

ID numbers, phone numbers, emails and dates of birth are **never sent to the AI**; they are filled on the server.

## Extra instructions

The box **Extra instructions** accepts a note for the AI, for example "Use the Swahili version of the headings".
