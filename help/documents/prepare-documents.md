**Prepare documents** is on every case. It uses the company's saved details and the official Word templates. No AI is involved: the same answers always give the same documents.

## Steps

1. On the case, find the **Prepare documents** card. The wizard that fits the case is marked **Suggested**.
2. Choose a wizard and answer the questions: who, which dates, amounts. Questions with a red star are required.
3. Press **Check** to see the list of documents and any **warnings** (for example "fewer than 2 directors would be left").
4. Press **Generate**. Each document gets a link to open it. They are also attached to the case checklist.

## The built-in wizards

| Wizard | Makes |
|---|---|
| **Change of directors** | Members' resolution, directors' resolution, and a Form 213a or 213b for each person |
| **Change of company secretary** | Form 213a and Form 213b |
| **Call on shares, forfeiture and re-allotment** | The resolutions and notices, and Forms 57a and 57b |
| **Annual return** | Form 131 from the saved company details |

## Good to know

- Your answers are saved on the case, so you can come back and change one.
- Generating again **replaces** the same files instead of piling up copies.
- If a person's details are missing, the wizard lists exactly what to add.
- When a date is left empty the wizard uses a sensible default (for example 30 days for a payment) and shows it in the document for you to check.

> The team's job is to **cross-check and sign**. Always read the documents before they go to BRELA.

Managers can change the questions and add wizards: [Edit the wizards](help:settings/document-wizards).
