## Questions

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Transfer 1 to 4: seller, buyer, number of shares** | Transfer 1 is required. The seller is picked from the company's shareholders; the buyer can be any saved person or a new one. A transfer that is not complete is left out and flagged. |
| **Articles governing the form and registration of transfers** (required) | Take it from the company's own Articles, for example "articles 22 to 27 of the Company's Articles of Association". |
| **Date of the share transfer agreements** (required) | |
| **Date the company received the agreements, instruments and TRA documents** (required) | |
| **Directors' meeting: date** (required) | On or after the date the documents were received. |
| **Time** | Default 09:00. |
| **Chairman of the meeting** | Defaults to the first director. |
| **The transfer instruments are duly stamped and the TRA clearance is in hand** (required) | Tick to confirm. |

The notice and quorum provisions of the Articles are asked once and remembered ([How document filling works](help:documents/how-document-filling-works)).

## Documents made

- **Directors' resolution: transfer of shares**, with the directors present, the transfers and the filing details.

## Warnings

- "Transfer 1 is incomplete": a transfer needs the seller, the buyer and the number of shares.
- "The seller and the buyer are the same person."
- "… holds N shares but is transferring M": the seller does not hold enough shares. Correct the shareholders or the number.
- "The directors' meeting is before the date the documents were received."

## Tips

- After a transfer, the annual return (Form 131) and, where the owners change, the beneficial-owner forms (15b, 15c, 15f) must be updated.
- Check the Articles' transfer procedure (for example pre-emption rights or director approval) before you generate.
- After the transfer, update the shareholders and their shares on the **Company** tab so the annual return is correct.
