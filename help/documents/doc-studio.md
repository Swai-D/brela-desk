**Doc Studio** is where you finish a document: change the text (bold, italic, underline), place your signature, and export a Word or PDF file with real fonts.

Open it from the menu, or from a document on a case. Your saved signatures are in the **My signatures** panel; both side panels can be collapsed for more room.
