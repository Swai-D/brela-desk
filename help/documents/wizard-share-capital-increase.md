## Questions

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Amount of the increase** (required) | A number. |
| **Existing nominal capital** | Empty uses the share capital saved on the **Company** tab. |
| **Article that authorises the company to increase its share capital** (required) | Take it from the company's own Articles. |
| **Condition (a) of the new shares** (required) | Rights of the new shares. |
| **Condition (b), if any** / **Condition (c), if any** | Left out when empty. |
| **Members' meeting: date** (required) | The date of the meeting. |
| **Time** | Default 10:00. |
| **Chairman of the meeting** | Defaults to the first director. |
| **Notice of the meeting** | **Full notice (not less than 21 days)** or **Shorter notice agreed by the members**. |
| **Full notice: date it was given. Shorter notice: date the members agreed** (required) | Goes into the notice paragraph of the resolution. |
| **Director who signs the form** | Defaults to the first director. |
| **Date the director signs** | Defaults to the meeting date. |

## Documents made

- **Members' special resolution: increase of share capital.** It shows the existing and the new total capital.
- **Form 68: increase in nominal capital.**

These resolutions are drafted from the house style and the Companies Act, and are marked in the package as pending the senior's review. Read each one once before the team relies on it.

## Warnings

- "Full notice must be at least 21 days before the meeting." Choose shorter notice if the members agreed in writing, or move the meeting date.
- "The notice date is after the meeting date."
- "The company has no saved share capital": type the existing capital in the wizard or correct the **Company** tab.
- A reminder of the date by which notice must reach the Registrar (30 days after the resolution).
- A member with no shares recorded: correct it on the **Company** tab, or the resolution shows 0 shares.

## Tips

- The resolution is drafted as a special resolution. Confirm the type required by the company's Articles.
- Send Form 68 with a printed copy of the resolution within 30 days.
