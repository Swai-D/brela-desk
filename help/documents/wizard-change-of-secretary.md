## Questions

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| **New secretary** | Pick a saved person or add a new one. |
| **End the current secretary's appointment** | Tick when the current secretary leaves. |
| **Director who signs the forms** | Defaults to the first director. |
| **Effective date** (required) | The date of the appointment or termination. |

## Documents made

- **Form 213a: appointment of secretary** for the new secretary (the role on the form is **SECRETARY**).
- **Form 213b: termination of secretary** for the person leaving.

## Warnings

- "Choose a new secretary, or tick that the current secretary's appointment ends."
- "The company has no saved secretary to end." Add the current secretary to the company first and give them the role **Secretary** ([People](help:clients/people)).

## Tips

- Both forms are made when you appoint a new one and end the old one in the same case.
- Check the new secretary's date of birth, nationality, occupation and address before generating.
