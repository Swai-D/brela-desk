## Questions

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| **New company name** (required) | Exactly as reserved with BRELA, including LIMITED. |
| **The new name is reserved with BRELA** (required) | Tick to confirm. The name must be reserved before the form is filed. |
| **Members' meeting that passes the special resolution: date** (required) | The date of the meeting. It is also the date on Form 33a. |
| **Time** | Default 10:00. |
| **Chairman of the meeting** | Defaults to the first director. |
| **Notice of the meeting** | **Full notice (not less than 21 days)** or **Shorter notice agreed by the members**. |
| **Full notice: date it was given. Shorter notice: date the members agreed** (required) | Goes into the notice paragraph of the resolution. |
| **Director who signs the form** | Defaults to the first director. |
| **Date the director signs** | Defaults to the resolution date. |

## Documents made

- **Members' special resolution: change of company name.** It lists the members present with their shares, records the notice, the three-fourths majority (section 146) and the change of name subject to the Registrar's written approval (section 33), and authorises a director to lodge Form 33a within 14 days.
- **Form 33a: change of company name.** The old name is taken from the **Company** tab.

The resolution is drafted from the house style and the Companies Act, and is marked in the package as pending the senior's review. Read it once before the team relies on it.

## Warnings

- "The new name is the same as the current name."
- "Full notice must be at least 21 days before the meeting." Choose shorter notice if the members agreed in writing, or move the meeting date.
- "The notice date is after the meeting date."
- A member with no shares recorded: correct it on the **Company** tab, or the resolution shows 0 shares.
- "The new name does not end with LIMITED, LTD or PLC." Check it against the name reserved with BRELA.
- "The company has no saved name, so the old name on the form will be empty."

## Tips

- Attach the signed special resolution to Form 33a. Then wait for the Registrar's written approval and give notice within 14 days.
- Once BRELA accepts the change, update the name on the **Company** tab.
