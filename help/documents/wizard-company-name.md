## Questions

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| **New company name** (required) | Exactly as reserved with BRELA, including LIMITED. |
| **The new name is reserved with BRELA** (required) | Tick to confirm. The name must be reserved before the form is filed. |
| **Date of the resolution approving the change** (required) | The date of the resolution you attach. |
| **Director who signs the form** | Defaults to the first director. |
| **Date the director signs** | Defaults to the resolution date. |

## Documents made

- **Form 33a: change of company name.** The old name is taken from the **Company** tab.

## Warnings

- "The new name is the same as the current name."
- "The new name does not end with LIMITED, LTD or PLC." Check it against the name reserved with BRELA.
- "The company has no saved name, so the old name on the form will be empty."

## Tips

- A copy of the resolution must be attached. The app has no template for it yet, so prepare it separately.
- Once BRELA accepts the change, update the name on the **Company** tab.
