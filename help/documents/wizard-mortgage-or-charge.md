## Documents made

- **Directors' resolution: approval of mortgage or charge**, which also authorises the entry in the register of charges.
- **Form 99: particulars of mortgage or charge.**

These resolutions are drafted from the house style and the Companies Act, and are marked in the package as pending the senior's review. Read each one once before the team relies on it.

## Warnings

- "The directors' meeting is after the date the charge is created."
- "The amount secured is not a number."
- "More than 42 days have passed since the charge was created": the charge may be void against a liquidator, administrator or creditor (section 99). Ask the court to extend the time (section 108).
- A member with no shares recorded: correct it on the **Company** tab, or the resolution shows 0 shares.

## Tips

- Deliver Form 99 with the original instrument to the Registrar within 42 days of creating the charge.
