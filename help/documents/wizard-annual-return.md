## Questions

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Return made up to** | Default: the latest anniversary of incorporation |
| **There were no charges in the period** | Tick when true |
| **A list of changes is enclosed** | Tick if you attach one |
| **A full list of members is enclosed** | Tick if you attach one |

## What is filled

Company name and number, registered office, return date, the share capital and **shares issued**, the **aggregate nominal value**, and the **members** (alphabetical, name and address, shares). The more complete the **Company** tab, the less you type.

## Before you generate

1. Open the client's **Company** tab and check the shareholders and their shares add up to the issued shares.
2. Check the registered office and the nominal value.
3. If anything was missing, import again from BRELA ([Import](help:clients/import-from-brela)).

Then press **Generate** and open the Form 131 with the **Word** button (to edit) or the **PDF** button (to upload). Check the totals and the members.

See also: [How document filling works](help:documents/how-document-filling-works).
