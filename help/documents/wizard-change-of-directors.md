Use it when new directors join, directors leave, or both.

## Questions

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Directors being appointed** | Up to two. Pick saved people or **Add a new person**. Their details feed Form 213a. |
| **Directors resigning** | Pick from the current directors. |
| **Chairman of the meetings** | Defaults to the first director who stays. |
| **The appointment takes effect** | **When the new director's signed consent is received** (standard) or **From the date of the resolution, no condition**. Controls the words "with effect from the time of receipt of that consent". |
| **The resignations take effect** | **At the conclusion of the meeting** (standard) or **No time stated**. |
| **Members' meeting: date** (required) and **Time** | Time defaults to 10:00. |
| **Directors' meeting accepting the resignations: date** and **Time** | Default: same day as the members' meeting, 11:00. |
| **Articles of Association references** | Notice of directors' meetings, directors' quorum (in words and the article), shorter notice for a general meeting, members' quorum, the article for the number of directors, the article adopting Table A. Saved on the client and reused. |

## Documents made

| Document | When |
|---|---|
| **Members' resolution: appointment of directors** | Someone is appointed |
| **Directors' resolution: resignation of directors** | Someone resigns |
| **Form 213a: appointment** | One for each person appointed |
| **Form 213b: termination** | One for each person resigning |

The resolutions list the attendees (the members and the directors who stay), the chairman, the shares shown for members, and the filing deadline.

## Warnings

- A member saved with no shares: the members' resolution would show 0 shares. Correct it on the **Company** tab.

- "Choose who is being appointed or who is resigning."
- "After these changes the company would have N directors. A private company needs at least 2 (s.189 of the Companies Act), so appoint before anyone resigns."
- "The directors' meeting accepting the resignations must be on or after the members' meeting that appoints the new directors."
- People listed with missing details or "not saved yet".

## Tips

1. Add the new directors as people first (with date of birth, occupation, nationality, address), or use **Add a new person** in the wizard.
2. If a person appears in the resignations and is also a shareholder or secretary, the resolution notes what they keep.
3. After generating, read Form 213a for each new director: names split into first names and surname, date of birth, occupation, address.
