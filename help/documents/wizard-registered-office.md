## Questions

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| **New registered office: plot, street and ward** (required) | Written in capitals on the form. |
| **P.O. Box (second line)** | Optional; left out when empty. |
| **Town or city** (required) | For example Dar es Salaam. |
| **Company e-mail address** (required) | Checked for a valid shape. |
| **Contact phone** (required) | |
| **The change takes effect on** (required) | The date of the move. |
| **Director who signs the form** | Defaults to the first director. |
| **Date the director signs** | Defaults to the effective date. |
| **Directors' meeting approving the change: date** (required) | On or before the date the change takes effect. |
| **Time** | Default 09:00. |
| **Chairman of the meeting** | Defaults to the first director. |
| **Article governing the registered office and the directors' power to change it** (required) | Take it from the company's own Articles, for example "Article 3 of the Company's Articles of Association". |
| **The directors' resolution is signed and ready to attach to Form 114** (required) | Tick to confirm that the signed resolution will go with the form. |

The notice and quorum provisions of the Articles are asked once and remembered ([How document filling works](help:documents/how-document-filling-works)).

## Documents made

- **Directors' resolution: change of registered office.** It records the old and the new office, the date it takes effect, the Article relied on (sections 113 and 114 of the Companies Act) and authorises a director to lodge Form 114 within 14 days.
- **Form 114: change of registered office**, with the company name, number and the new address.

The resolution is drafted from the house style and the Companies Act, and is marked in the package as pending the senior's review. Read it once before the team relies on it.

## Warnings

- "The form is signed after the date the change takes effect." BRELA expects the filing within 14 days of the change.
- "The directors' meeting is after the date the change takes effect."
- "The e-mail address does not look right."
- "The company has no saved director to sign the form." Add a director on the **Company** tab ([People](help:clients/people)).

## Tips

- After the move, update the registered office on the **Company** tab so later documents use the new address.
