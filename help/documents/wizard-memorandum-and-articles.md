## Questions

| Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Registered office is in** | **Tanzania Mainland** or **Tanzania Zanzibar**. |
| **Objects (one per line: ISIC class name and code)** (required) | For example "Computer programming activities (6201)". |
| **Authorised share capital** | Empty uses the share capital saved on the **Company** tab. |
| **Total number of shares** | Empty uses the number of shares saved on the **Company** tab. |
| **Nominal value of each share** | Empty uses the saved share value, or capital divided by the number of shares. |
| **First subscriber (a person)** (required) | Chosen from the shareholders on the **Company** tab; the name and address come from there. |
| **Shares taken by the first subscriber** (required) | |
| **Second subscriber: company name**, **address**, **director who signs**, **shares taken** | For a corporate subscriber. Leave all four empty if there is none. |
| **Drawn by: name** (required), **role**, **P.O. Box number** (required), **city** | Role defaults to SUBSCRIBER and city to Dar es Salaam. |

## Documents made

- **Memorandum and Articles of Association**, in the senior's own Word template. The company name, objects, share capital and both subscriber tables (Memorandum and Articles) are filled. The incorporation date and certificate number stay blank until BRELA registers the company.

The five highlighted alternative clauses in the Articles (term of office, resignation notice, officers and their terms) stay yellow so you choose them yourself.

## Warnings

- "The company has no saved share capital or number of shares": enter them here or on the **Company** tab.
- "The subscribers take more shares than the total number of shares."
- "Authorised capital is not the number of shares times the nominal value."
- "No second subscriber was entered. Delete the second subscriber row in both tables in Word." Also shown when the second subscriber is incomplete.
- "The company has no shareholders saved."

## Tips

- The template has two subscriber rows. For more subscribers, add rows in Word.
- Only the highlighted labels were renamed in the senior's template so the wizard can fill them; the wording is unchanged.
