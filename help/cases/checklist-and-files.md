Every case has a list of documents it needs.

- **Upload** a file to a checklist item (max 15 MB each). The item is ticked as soon as a file is attached.
- **Preview** Word and PDF files in the page without downloading.
- Documents made by **Prepare documents** are attached to the matching item automatically (for example Form 213a).
- Senior and Manager can rename or delete any file; other people only their own uploads.

> Documents that arrive on WhatsApp land in the [WhatsApp Inbox](help:whatsapp/inbox), where you attach them to the right case.
