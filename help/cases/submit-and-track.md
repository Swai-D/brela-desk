## Submit

1. When all documents are ready, move the case to **Ready to Submit** and have someone check it.
2. A **Senior** or **Manager** presses **Submit to BRELA** after submitting on the client's BRELA account (the vault has the login).
3. Save the **track number** in the field at the top of the case.

## BRELA answers

- **Approved:** record it. The case is **Completed**, and the Manager is alerted to send the report to the client. Upload BRELA's approval documents first.
- **Returned:** record it with a **reason**. The case goes to **Returned (Correction)**. Fix the documents, comment on what changed, and send it again.
