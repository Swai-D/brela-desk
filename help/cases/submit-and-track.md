## Before you submit

1. All checklist items are ticked and a Senior has checked every document.
2. The case is in **Ready to Submit**.

## Submit

1. Open the client's BRELA account. The login is in the **Client vault** card (press the lock button; it hides after 15 seconds).
2. Submit the application on BRELA's site.
3. On the case press **Submit to BRELA** in **What to do now**. Only a Senior or Manager can.
4. Type the **BRELA track number** in the field at the top and press the check button ("Saved ✓"). If another case has the same number the app warns you; check it is not a copy-paste mistake.

## When BRELA answers

| Answer | What you press | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Approved | **BRELA approved ✓** | **Completed**. Managers are alerted to send the report. Upload BRELA's approval documents to the case first. |
| Returned | **BRELA returned ↺** and write the reason | **Returned (Correction)**, the round counter goes up, the assignee and Managers are alerted |

After a return: fix the documents, comment what changed, press **Corrections done** (back to Ready to Submit), and submit again.

## After completion

Send the final report and documents to the client and write a comment saying they received them.
