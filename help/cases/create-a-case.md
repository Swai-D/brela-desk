---
title: Create a case
summary: Open a case for a client, choose the case type and what is changing.
keywords: new case, case type, change of particulars, options, assign, due date, track number
order: 1
---
1. Press **New case** (on the Case Board, My Work or the client page).
2. Choose the **client** and the **case type**.
3. For **Change of Particulars**, choose what is changing (for example directors, registered office, company name) and the action (add, remove, change). The checklist is built from your choices, and you can add another change later.
4. Assign the person and set a due date if needed, then create.

The case opens with its **checklist of documents**, **What to do now**, comments and the **Prepare documents** card.

> A Manager can change the case type and the checklists in [Settings](help:settings/case-types). A Manager can delete a case only after typing its code.
