The **Comments** card is where the team talks about a case so nothing is lost in private chats.

## Write

1. Type in "Write a comment..." at the bottom of the card.
2. Press Enter or the send button. Empty comments are refused ("Write something").

Comments show newest first with the author and the time. The card pages through them ten at a time and shows the total.

## Edit and delete

- You can **edit only your own** comments: "You can edit only your own comments."
- You can **delete only your own**; a **Manager** can delete any comment.
- Deleting cannot be undone and is written in the [audit log](help:settings/audit-log).

## Good practice

- When a case is **returned**, write what you changed.
- Record who the client spoke to and when.
- Never paste passwords here; use the [vault](help:clients/vault).

Comments are also found by [Search](help:daily-work/search).
