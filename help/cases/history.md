title: Case history
summary: The audit trail of a case: every move, upload, comment and change with who and when.
keywords: history, activity, audit, who did, log, timeline, entries, page

---
The **History** card lists everything that happened on the case, newest first: stage moves ("moved ready → submitted"), returns with reason, uploads, ticks, comments, assignments, generated documents ("filled …").

- It pages ten at a time and shows the total entries.
- The Dashboard's **Recent activity** shows the same entries across all cases.
- History cannot be edited or deleted by users, so it is a reliable record.

Use it to answer "who changed this?" or "when was it submitted?". For deletions and sensitive office-wide changes the Manager has the [audit log](help:settings/audit-log).
