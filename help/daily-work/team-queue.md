Under your own three lists in [My Work](help:daily-work/my-work), some people also see a card called **Team queue for your role**, followed by the name of your role in brackets. It helps you spot work that belongs to your role but is held by someone else.

## Who sees it
Only people whose **role** has the setting **Show the team queue in My Work for this role** switched on. A Manager sets this when editing the role (see [Team and roles](help:settings/team-and-roles) and [Edit the Playbook](help:daily-work/edit-the-playbook)). If your My Work page has no such card, your role does not have it. This is normal and not an error.

## What is listed
The queue shows cases that meet **all** of these:

1. The case is in a stage that **belongs to your role** in the [Playbook](help:daily-work/playbook) (for example, a Senior role owns Ready to Submit).
2. The case is **assigned to someone else**, not you.
3. The case is not Completed, or it was completed **in the last 7 days**.

The card says: "Cases in stages that belong to your role, held by others. Completed cases show for 7 days." A number badge at the top right gives the total.

Cases are grouped under a stage heading (the coloured stage badge and a small count). Each row shows the client, case code, case type, track number if there is one, and **with** the name of the person who holds it. Press a row to open the case.

If nothing qualifies you see **Nothing waiting for your role**.

## How to use it
- Check it when your own list is empty, or when a colleague is away.
- Open a case and read **What to do now**. If the step is yours, the page says **This step is yours**. You can comment or help even when it is not.
- To take over a case, a Senior or Manager changes the assignee on the case page.

## Common problems

- **I expected a case here but it is not listed.** The stage must belong to your role in the Playbook. Look at the **How a case flows** tab to see which roles own each stage. Also, cases already assigned to you are in your own lists, not here.
- **A Completed case is gone.** After 7 days it leaves the queue. Use [Search](help:daily-work/search).
- **I want the queue but do not have it.** Ask a Manager to tick the team queue setting on your role.
