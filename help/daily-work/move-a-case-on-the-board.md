You can change a case's stage straight from the [Case Board](help:daily-work/case-board) by **dragging** its card into another column. The same change can be made with the stage buttons on the case page.

## Steps
1. Open the **Case Board**.
2. Press and hold a card (not the client name, which is a link) and drag it onto another column.
3. Release. A green message says **Case moved to ...** and the card stays in its new column.

If the move is refused, a red message appears and the card jumps back to where it was. Nothing was changed.

## Moving to Returned (Correction)
Dropping a card into **Returned (Correction)** opens a box titled **BRELA returned this case** asking **What did BRELA ask to correct?** (example text: "ID copy was not signed"). Write the reason and press **Save**. The reason is shown on the card, in [My Work](help:daily-work/my-work) and on the [Dashboard](help:daily-work/dashboard), and the case's **BRELA round** goes up by one. If you close the box without writing a reason, the card is not moved.

## Who can move what
| Move to | Who | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| New, Gathering Docs | Staff, Senior, Manager | None |
| Ready to Submit | Staff, Senior, Manager | Every checklist document must be ready |
| Submitted to BRELA | **Senior or Manager** | Every checklist document must be ready |
| Completed | **Senior or Manager** | Every checklist document must be ready |
| Returned (Correction) | **Senior or Manager** | A reason is required |

## Messages you may see
| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Complete all documents in the checklist first.** | Open the case and tick or upload the missing documents. See [Checklist and files](help:cases/checklist-and-files). |
| **Only a Senior or Manager can do this step.** | Ask a Senior or Manager. See [Roles and access](help:getting-started/roles-and-access). |
| **Write what BRELA asked to correct.** | Try again and give a reason. |

## Good to know
- Moving a case **out** of Returned clears the red correction note. Moving into Completed records the completion date, which the [Monthly report](help:daily-work/reports) uses.
- Moving to **Ready to Submit** sends a WhatsApp alert to Seniors and Managers, and Returned or Completed alert the Managers, when WhatsApp alerts are set up.
- Every move is saved in the case's history with your name.
- The track number is saved on the case page, not on the board. See [Submit and track](help:cases/submit-and-track).

> Moved the wrong card? Drag it back, unless it needs a Senior step. If you cannot, ask a Senior or Manager to move it.
