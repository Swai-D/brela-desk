The **Playbook** is the team handbook inside the app. It has three tabs.

## My role

Your role name, your access level, and a summary of the role. Below it:

- **What you can do** and **What you cannot do (ask a Senior or Manager)**. The lists change with your access level.
- Your responsibilities as written by the Manager.

## How a case flows

The six stages in order, each with:

- the roles responsible (your own role is highlighted),
- the steps to follow,
- the **Done when** rule that tells you the stage is finished.

The same text appears on every case in **What to do now**.

## Everyone's responsibilities

One card per role with its access level, the number of active people, and its responsibilities.

## Editing the texts (Manager)

Managers see an edit button on each role card and on each stage.

1. Press the edit button.
2. Change the summary and the responsibilities (one per line), or for a stage the responsible roles, the steps and the **Done when** text.
3. Save.

The change shows for everyone immediately. Roles themselves (names, access levels) are managed in [Team and roles](help:settings/team-and-roles).

> The steps are advice, not locks. Hard rules (all documents ready, only a Senior submits, a reason for a return) are enforced by the app whatever the text says.
