The **Dashboard** is the first page after you sign in. It answers: "How is the office doing, and what needs me?"

## The greeting line

Under "Good morning, <name>" you see today's date and either a red link, **"N of your cases need action now"** (it opens [My Work](help:daily-work/my-work)), or the green text **nothing urgent on your list**. A case needs action when BRELA returned it or it is due today or late.

## The getting-started card

New users see a **Getting started** checklist above the cards. Its steps tick themselves; **Go** opens the page and **Guide** the help. Press **Dismiss** to hide it ([The checklist](help:getting-started/getting-started-checklist)).

## The five cards

| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Open cases** | Every case that is not Completed |
| **Submitted to BRELA** | Cases waiting for BRELA's answer |
| **Returned** | Cases BRELA sent back for correction |
| **Overdue** | Open cases past their due date (the card turns red when above zero) |
| **Completed** | Cases approved by BRELA |

The percentage on each card is its share of all cases.

## Cases by status

A coloured bar and a legend show how many cases are in each of the six stages. With no cases it says "No cases yet. Create the first one with New case."

## Needs attention

A list of cases that were **returned by BRELA** or are **overdue**, oldest due date first, five per page. Each row shows the client, the case code and type, the stage, how late it is, and who has it. Click a row to open the case. When the list is empty you see "Nothing needs attention".

## Workload

One bar per active team member: the number of **open** cases, and in red how many of them are **late**. Use it to decide who can take the next case.

## Recent activity

The latest actions on cases (who did what, on which case, how long ago), five per page. Click the case code to open it.

## The New case button

The button at the top opens the same dialog as everywhere else ([Create a case](help:cases/create-a-case)).

> The Dashboard shows the whole office. For your own list use [My Work](help:daily-work/my-work).
