Managers can rewrite the texts that every team member sees in the [Playbook](help:daily-work/playbook). Seniors and Staff see no edit buttons.

## Change a stage
1. Open **Playbook** and the tab **How a case flows**.
2. Press the small pencil button (**Edit stage**) at the top right of the stage block.
3. In the box **Edit stage: ...** choose the **Responsible roles** by ticking the roles.
4. Edit the **Steps**: one per line, in order.
5. Edit **Done when**.
6. Press **Save**. The page reloads with your changes.

What it changes:

- The steps and the "done when" text appear in the **What to do now** card on every case in that stage.
- The responsible roles decide who sees **This step is yours** on the case and which stages appear in the [team queue](help:daily-work/team-queue) of a role.

The steps are advice only. They never block anybody, so you can write them freely.

## Change a role
1. Open the tab **Everyone's responsibilities**.
2. Press the pencil (**Edit responsibilities**) on the role's card.
3. In **Edit role** you can change:
   - **Role name**
   - **What can this role do?**: the permission tick boxes, with **Start from** Staff, Senior, Manager or Nothing
   - **Show the team queue in My Work for this role**
   - **Summary**: one sentence for the Playbook
   - **Responsibilities**: one per line
4. Press **Save**.

Adding or deleting roles, and moving people between roles, is done in **Settings**. See [Team and roles](help:settings/team-and-roles). A link to Settings is shown at the top of the tab.

## Tips for good texts
- Write each step as an action: "Check the director's ID is signed".
- Keep "Done when" to one sentence a new person can test.
- Use the same words as the app buttons (for example **Ready to Submit**).

## Common problems

- **My change does not show for a colleague.** They need to reload the page.
- **I cannot find the pencil buttons.** You must be a Manager. Check the badge on the **My role** tab.
- **I changed an access level by mistake.** Edit the role again and set it back. The effect on the people in that role is immediate. See [Roles and access](help:getting-started/roles-and-access).
- **Saved lines disappeared.** Empty lines are removed when saving, and at most 30 lines are kept per list.
