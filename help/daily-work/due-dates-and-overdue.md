Every case can have a **due date**. The app uses it to warn you before a case becomes a problem. This guide explains the rules in plain words.

## When is a case "late"?
A case is **late** (also called **overdue**) when **both** are true:

- its stage is **not Completed**, and
- its due date is **before today**.

A case due **today** is not late yet, but the app already treats it as urgent. A case with **No date** is never late, so give every case a date.

## Where you see it

| Place | What you see |
|---|---|
| [Dashboard](help:daily-work/dashboard) | The **Overdue** card, the **Needs attention** list ("3 days late") and "late" under a person in **Team workload** |
| [My Work](help:daily-work/my-work) | Late and due-today cases go into **Do now**; the row says "Due today" or "2 days late" |
| [Case Board](help:daily-work/case-board) | A red edge and "**3d late**" on the card, a red **late** count on the column, and the **Overdue** quick filter |
| Profile | The **Overdue** card counts your own late cases |
| Bell | The red number includes your own cases that are Returned or late. See [Notifications](help:account/notifications) |
| [Monthly report](help:daily-work/reports) | **Open now** and the number overdue (Managers) |

In the case list, the wording is "In 4 days", "Due today", "1 day late", "No date" or "Completed".

## Messages that come to you
- **WhatsApp daily digest.** At about **07:00 East Africa Time, Monday to Saturday**, each person who has a WhatsApp number on their profile gets a message listing their cases that are returned by BRELA, overdue, due today or due tomorrow (up to 6 cases, then "+N more"). This works only when the office has WhatsApp alerts set up.
- **Escalation.** When a case is **more than 3 days overdue**, the person it is assigned to and all Managers get a bell notification such as "C-0123 (Client) is 5 days late - escalated". It repeats at most **once a week** until the case is fixed or completed.

## Who can change a due date
A **Senior or Manager** changes a case's due date or assignee on the case page. Staff can set the date only when creating the case. If a date is wrong and you are Staff, ask a Senior or Manager.

## Common problems

- **A case shows late but it is due today in Tanzania.** The page compares dates using your computer's clock. Check that your computer's date and time are correct. Around midnight, the day can differ by a few hours.
- **A completed case is red.** Completed cases are never late. Reload the page.
- **Too many "late" cases.** Late counts only fall when a case is completed or its due date is moved forward by a Senior or Manager. Do not complete a case early just to clear the list; completing needs BRELA's approval.
- **I get no WhatsApp digest.** Check your phone number on your [Profile](help:account/profile), and ask a Manager whether WhatsApp is set up. See [WhatsApp Inbox](help:whatsapp/inbox).

> Set the due date a few days **before** BRELA's real deadline, so the "Due today" warning still leaves time to act.
