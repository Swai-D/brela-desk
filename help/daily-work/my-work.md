**My Work** lists the open cases assigned to you and puts the urgent ones first.

## The three groups

| Group | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Do now** | Cases BRELA returned, or due today, or late |
| **In progress** | Cases you are preparing (New, Gathering Docs, Ready to Submit) that are not urgent |
| **Waiting on BRELA** | Every case in the **Submitted to BRELA** stage, for the **whole team**. Yours come first; a case that belongs to a colleague shows "with <name>". Nothing to do until BRELA answers. |

The number on each group is its count (Waiting on BRELA counts the whole team's submitted cases, not only yours). A submitted case of yours that is due today or late shows under **Do now** instead. At the top you see your name, how many cases need action now, and your open cases. "Nothing urgent" in green means you are clear.

Each row shows the client, the case code and type, the stage, the due date or how late it is, and a small progress bar of documents ready (for example 3/5). Click a row to open the case.

## Your role card

Below the hero card a short text from the [Playbook](help:daily-work/playbook) reminds you of the responsibilities of your role. Roles with a **team queue** (set by the Manager) also see the cases of their stage that nobody has taken yet.

## How a case gets to My Work

A case appears here when it is **assigned to you**. The Manager or a Senior assigns it when creating the case, or later with **Edit case** ([Edit a case](help:cases/edit-a-case)). You also get a WhatsApp message if your phone is saved in your [profile](help:account/profile).

## If a case is missing

- It may be assigned to someone else: ask a Senior or Manager to reassign it.
- It may be **Completed**: finished cases leave My Work (the team queue shows them for 7 days).
- Use the [Case Board](help:daily-work/case-board) filters to see all cases.

See also: [How a case flows](help:getting-started/how-a-case-flows).
