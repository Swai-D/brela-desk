**Reports** opens the **Monthly report**: how the team handled cases in a chosen month, compared with the month before. Only a **Manager** can open it. The menu item is hidden for others, and anyone else who types the address sees **Only a Manager can open Reports.**

## Choose the month
Under the title you have **left and right arrows**, a month box, and a **This month** button. The page opens on the current month. You cannot go past the current month (the right arrow is greyed out).

## The six number cards
| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Received** | Cases created in the month |
| **Completed** | Cases moved to Completed in the month |
| **Returned by BRELA** | Number of times a case was marked as returned in the month |
| **Avg turnaround** | Average days from received to completed, for cases completed in the month |
| **First-time approval** | Percent of those completed cases that were never returned by BRELA |
| **Open now** | Cases not completed today, with how many are overdue |

Under the first three cards a small arrow compares with last month, for example **5 vs last month**. Green means better (more Completed, fewer returns); red means worse; Received is neutral grey.

## Charts and tables
- **Monthly activity**: received, submitted, completed and BRELA returns against the previous month.
- **Cases by type** and **Average turnaround by type**.
- **Team activity**: received, completed and returned per person.
- **Top clients**: top 10 by received and completed cases.
- **BRELA correction reasons**: how often each written reason was used.
- **Team completion performance**: one round gauge per person. **Rate = cases assigned and completed in this month divided by cases assigned in this month.** The gauge is green from 80%, amber from 50% and red below 50%. If a case was reassigned, only the last person who held it when it was completed gets the credit.
- **Completed cases**: table with Case, Track no., Client, Type, Handled by, Received, Completed, Days, Returns.
- **BRELA corrections**: table with Date, Case, Client, Reason.

## Print or export
- **Print / Save PDF** opens the print window. Choose **Save as PDF** as the printer to keep a copy. The page adds a title "BRELA Desk - Monthly report".
- **Download CSV** saves a file named like `brela-report-2026-11.csv` that opens in Excel, with the summary, tables by type and person, completed cases and corrections.

## Common problems

- **All zeros.** Nothing happened in that month, or you chose a month before the team started using the app.
- **Avg turnaround shows a dash.** No case was completed in the month.
- **A case is missing from Completed.** It counts in the month it was moved to Completed, not when it was created. Moving a case back out of Completed removes the date.

> Months are cut at midnight in Tanzania (East Africa Time).
