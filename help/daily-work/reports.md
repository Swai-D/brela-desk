**Reports** opens the **Monthly report**: how the team handled cases in a chosen month, compared with the month before. Only a **Manager** can open it. The menu item is hidden for others, and anyone else who types the address sees **Only a Manager can open Reports.**

## Choose the month
At the top right you have **left and right arrows**, a month box, and a **This month** button. The page opens on the current month. You cannot go past the current month (the right arrow is greyed out). Under the title you see, for example, "November 2026 · compared with October 2026".

## The summary
The report opens with a short written summary of the month, for example: how many cases the team received, sent to BRELA and completed, how many times BRELA sent work back and the most common reason, and how many cases are open now and overdue. Beside it are three figures:

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Average turnaround** | Average days from received to completed, for cases completed in the month |
| **First-time approval** | Percent of those completed cases that BRELA never sent back |
| **Open now** | Cases not completed today, with how many are overdue |

## Activity
Four bars: **Received** (cases created in the month), **Submitted to BRELA**, **Completed** (cases moved to Completed in the month) and **Returned by BRELA** (times a case was marked as returned). The coloured bar is this month; the thin black **tick** marks last month. To the right a small chip compares with last month, for example **+5 vs Oct**. Green means better (more Completed, fewer returns), red means worse, and grey is neutral. Hover over a bar to see both numbers.

## Team
One row per person with **Received**, **Completed**, **Returned**, **Completion rate** and **Open now** (with an **overdue** note in red). **Completion rate = cases assigned and completed in this month divided by cases assigned in this month.** If a case was reassigned, only the last person who held it when it was completed gets the credit.

## Case types
Each case type with **Received**, **Completed**, **Returned** and **Average turnaround** in days.

## Top clients and BRELA corrections
- **Top clients**: up to 10 clients. The blue bar is cases received and the green bar is cases completed.
- **BRELA corrections**: the written reasons BRELA gave, most common first, with how many times each was used. Under it the **Correction log** lists each return with the case, client, date and reason.

## Completed cases
A table with Case (click the code to open the case), Track no., Client, Type, Handled by, Received, Completed, Days and Returns.

## Print or export
- **Print / Save PDF** opens the print window. Choose **Save as PDF** as the printer to keep a copy. The printout drops the buttons and keeps the summary, bars and tables.
- **Download CSV** saves a file named like `brela-report-2026-11.csv` that opens in Excel, with the summary, tables by type and person, completed cases and corrections.

## Common problems

- **All zeros.** Nothing happened in that month, or you chose a month before the team started using the app.
- **Avg turnaround shows a dash.** No case was completed in the month.
- **A case is missing from Completed.** It counts in the month it was moved to Completed, not when it was created. Moving a case back out of Completed removes the date.

> Months are cut at midnight in Tanzania (East Africa Time).
